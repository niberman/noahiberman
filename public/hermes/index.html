Hermes agent

Private, personal automation

Hermes is Noah Berman's self-hosted assistant. It connects to Google only to perform tasks Noah requests in his own Gmail and Google Calendar accounts.

Purpose

Hermes helps its owner read, organize, draft, and manage personal email and calendar information. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide access to anyone else's Google data.

How access works

OperatorNoah Berman
AccessOnly after Noah grants Google OAuth permission
ControlNoah can revoke access at any time in his Google Account
Owner-controlled. Hermes uses Google data only for Noah's requested personal workflows. It does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.
