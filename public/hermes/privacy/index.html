Hermes privacy policy

Effective September 20, 2026

This policy explains how Hermes, Noah Berman's self-hosted personal assistant, handles Google user data.

Data Hermes accesses

When Noah grants permission, Hermes may access information in Noah's Gmail and Google Calendar accounts that is needed for requested email and calendar workflows.

  • Gmail messages, threads, labels, and drafts needed to search, summarize, organize, or prepare replies.
  • Google Calendar events and related details needed to review or manage Noah's schedule.
  • Basic Google account information needed to identify the authorized account.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the functions Noah requests. Hermes does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

Storage and security

Hermes runs on Noah's own computer. OAuth credentials and workflow data are kept in Noah-controlled systems and are limited to the access needed for those workflows. Access is protected by authentication and operating-system controls.

Sharing

Hermes does not disclose Google user data to third parties except when Noah explicitly directs a workflow to communicate with a recipient, or when disclosure is required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for Noah's requested workflows and local records. Noah can stop future access by revoking Hermes in his Google Account. He can request deletion of locally stored OAuth credentials and related workflow data by contacting the address below.

Your choices

Revoke access. Open your Google Account, go to Security, then Third-party apps and services, and remove Hermes.
